RULES OF THE REALM

Every adventurer must abide by the laws of Cursed Souls.

1. GENERAL CONDUCT

  • Treat all players and staff with respect.
  • No harassment, discrimination, or toxic behavior of any kind.
  • Keep the global chat appropriate and constructive.
  • Staff decisions are final. Appeals can be sent via the contact form.

2. GAMEPLAY

  • No use of third-party programs, macros, or exploits.
  • Bug exploiting is strictly forbidden. Report bugs through the contact form.
  • Multiboxing is limited to 2 accounts per player.
  • PvP is allowed in designated areas only (see the Wiki for details).

3. ECONOMY & TRADING

  • Real-money trading (RMT) is strictly prohibited.
  • Scamming other players will result in a permanent ban.
  • Market manipulation or price fixing is not tolerated.

4. NAMING POLICY

  • Character names must be lore-friendly and appropriate.
  • No offensive, political, or real-world references in names.
  • Staff may request a name change at their discretion.

5. PENALTIES

  • Warning — First minor offense.
  • Temporary Ban — Repeated violations or moderate offenses.
  • Permanent Ban — Severe violations, cheating, or RMT.

Rules may be updated at any time. Ignorance of the rules does not exempt from punishment.
